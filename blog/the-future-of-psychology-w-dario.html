The Future of Psychology w/ Dario Krpan

/ Psychology vs AI

Dr. Dario Krpan is an Associate Professor of Behavioural Science at London School of Economics. His research focuses on social and cognitive psychology, and behavioural change in response to new technology.

He recently published From Nexus to the Tower of Babel: On Truly Human Psychology in the Dawn of AI, and agreed to speak to me about it. I recommend you check out the full paper this weekend.
